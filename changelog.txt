Version: 1.1.0
Date: 22-10-2026
  Changes:
    - Removed the lab supply cluster requirement: research selection is now always force-wide, so science packs spread across different lab networks count as available.
    - Removed the per-technology repeat-policy and level-target buttons from technology rows; saved and imported repeat rules still apply.
    - In-transit science totals now refresh as a bounded resumable job.
  Info:
    - Lab supply groups remain in the Research Health diagnostics and affected-lab inspection.
    - Saved and imported cluster-mode settings are retired automatically.
    - Portal versioning continues from the 1.0.0 portal release; pre-portal internal version numbers are retired.

Version: 1.0.0
Date: 14-10-2026
  Features:
    - Research control center with strategy profiles and planning pause.
    - Per-science priorities, hysteresis thresholds, flow forecasts and starvation prediction.
    - Surface and lab-supply-cluster-aware science availability.
    - Queue science budgets, deficits, limiting-science reporting and completion estimates.
    - Per-technology repeat rules and maximum repeat levels.
    - Named plan presets and import/export strings.
    - Manual trigger objectives, multiplayer administrator locking and plan history.
    - Native time-sliced parallel research and optional Parallel Research integration mode.
    - Performance mode and German, French and Portuguese translations.
  Compatibility:
    - Runs on Factorio 2.1.
