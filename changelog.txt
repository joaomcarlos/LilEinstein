Version: 1.4.0
Date: 30-6-2026
  Features:
    - Added the research control center with strategy profiles and planning pause.
    - Added per-science priorities, hysteresis thresholds, flow forecasts and starvation prediction.
    - Added surface and lab-supply-cluster-aware science availability.
    - Added queue science budgets, deficits, limiting-science reporting and completion estimates.
    - Added per-technology repeat rules and maximum repeat levels.
    - Added named plan presets and import/export strings.
    - Added manual trigger objectives, multiplayer administrator locking and plan history.
    - Added native time-sliced parallel research and optional Parallel Research integration mode.
    - Added performance mode and initial German, French and Portuguese translations.
  Compatibility:
    - This package targets Factorio 2.0.77 and later 2.0 releases. Factorio 2.1 requires a separately validated package and 2.1 manifest.
